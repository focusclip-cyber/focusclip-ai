# FocusClip AI

## Mission

Help creators convert long videos into high-quality speaker-focused shorts.

---

## Problem

Current AI clipping tools create random clips.

Creators want:

- Only one speaker
- Educational clips
- Viral clips
- Emotional clips

without spending hours editing.

---

## Solution

FocusClip AI lets users:

1. Paste YouTube Link

2. AI detects all speakers

3. User selects one speaker

4. AI asks what type of clips they want

5. AI creates downloadable Shorts

---

## Target Users

- YouTubers
- Podcasters
- Coaches
- Teachers
- Webinar Hosts
- Agencies

---

## MVP

✅ Paste URL

✅ Detect Speakers

✅ Choose Speaker

✅ Generate Clip Suggestions
